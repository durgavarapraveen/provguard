# Code of conduct

provguard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@durgavarapraveen](https://github.com/durgavarapraveen)). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer can remove content, and warn or ban people who break the code.
